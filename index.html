The Friction

The One-Size-Fits-All Crisis in Academic Learning

Current digital educational environments assume a neurotypical reader with uniform cognitive stamina. For neurodivergent learners—specifically students diagnosed with ADHD, dyslexia, autism, and multilingual students—dense educational materials induce severe perceptual and cognitive breakdown.

🧠

1. Working Memory Fatigue

Dense, unbroken walls of academic prose lack structural pauses. Learners with ADHD experience rapid cognitive overload within 4 to 7 minutes of unsegmented reading, triggering attention fatigue and involuntary focus drift.

MindEase Solution: Non-destructive semantic chunking, progressive text reveals, and visual breathing anchors.
📐

2. Symbolic Parsing Barriers

Mathematical proofs and STEM formulas formatted as raw LaTeX strings or fragmented HTML break reading flow. For dyslexic readers, symbol orientation and spatial grouping create high visual friction.

MindEase Solution: Isolated KaTeX math typesetting with accessible MathML alt-text, bounded outlines, and Manim visual animations.
📖

3. Context-Free Vocabulary

When encountering unfamiliar scientific terminology, students must switch browser tabs to search for definitions. Every context switch shatters working memory and distracts from comprehension.

MindEase Solution: In-situ interactive [DEF] tooltips rendered directly over terms without leaving the paragraph.
🔍

4. Skim Blindspots

Students often skim through dense sections without registering key conceptual leaps. These blindspots remain invisible until high-stakes testing, leaving silent knowledge gaps.

MindEase Solution: Passive behavioral dwell tracking, knowledge gap severity alerts (Critical/Moderate/Minor), and session review cards.

System Design

The Three-Layer Cognitive Architecture

MindEase operates across five browser entrypoints coordinating through strongly-typed messaging. The system decouples presentation transformation, behavioral modeling, and session memory into three distinct, robust layers.

01
Layer 1

Real-Time Content Transformation

Intercepts academic pages, PDFs, and videos. The backend DeepSeek planner and Mistral generator tag paragraphs into semantic blocks without destroying source text.

  • Semantic Tagging: [CHUNK], [CONCEPT], [DEF], [FORMULA].
  • Shadow DOM Isolation: Encapsulated inside an open Shadow Root with all: initial to prevent host CSS collisions.
  • Multimodal Generation: KaTeX equations, Napkin AI vector diagrams, and Manim mathematical animations.
  • Dual-Modality Narration: Client-side sentence chunking with real-time visual karaoke highlighting.
▼
02
Layer 2

Adaptive Cognitive Profiling (RL Agent)

Models learner processing characteristics across 81 discrete behavioral states ($3^4$ combinations of Highlight, Pause, Re-read, and Skip rates) and 9 action spaces.

  • The Learning Puzzle: 8-dimension onboarding questionnaire (Visual/Text, Density, Pacing, Support Needs).
  • Strict User Agency: Manual user overrides always supersede model recommendations.
  • Zero Mid-Session Disruption: Telemetry is logged passively; adaptation parameters never jarringly mutate mid-paragraph.
  • Q-Learning Reward: Updates post-session based on cumulative engagement: $R = 2.0 \cdot \text{engaged} - 1.5 \cdot \text{skip} + 0.5 \cdot \text{highlight}$.
▼
03
Layer 3

Session Memory & Synthesis

Closes the learning loop by transforming passive web browsing into an active, reviewable knowledge base and structured study cards.

  • Multi-Tab Workspace: Tracks active vs. passive focus across multiple research papers and Wikipedia tabs.
  • Knowledge Gap Severity Ranking: Categorizes rushed or skipped concepts into Critical, Moderate, or Minor gaps.
  • 7-Section Knowledge Artifact: Synthesizes visited resources, key definitions, cross-source links, and Anki-ready flashcards.
  • Privacy & Data Sovereignty: Local-first IndexedDB storage with optional encrypted Supabase cloud synchronization.

Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (Vol. 42)

Combinatorial Search and Heuristic Optimization in State-Space Exploration

A. Mercer, K. Vance, et al. • Published 2026

Artificial intelligence problem solving in discrete domains fundamentally relies on state-space search architectures where the totality of achievable configurations is formally represented as a directed graph G = (V, E). When traversing combinatorial structures, naive exhaustive search incurs an exponential complexity penalty governed by O(b^d), where b represents the branching factor of the search graph and d signifies the minimal depth of the optimal goal state. In non-trivial decision domains such as automated theorem proving or game-tree evaluations, the combinatorial explosion renders blind traversal computationally intractable within standard computational horizons. Consequently, heuristic functions h(n) estimating the cost from candidate node n to the nearest goal state are introduced to prune suboptimal trajectories, transforming unguided exploration into an admissible A* search formulation whose temporal efficiency is strictly bounded by the informational quality and monotonicity of the employed heuristic estimator.

Furthermore, when transitions incorporate stochastic environmental dynamics, the representation generalizes to a Markov Decision Process defined by the 5-tuple (S, A, P, R, \gamma). Here, the optimal state-value policy V*(s) satisfies the recursive Bellman optimality equation: V*(s) = \max_{a \in A} \sum_{s' \in S} P(s' | s, a) [ R(s, a, s') + \gamma V*(s') ], requiring continuous convergence over iterative dynamic programming sweeps.

Scientific Integrity & Responsible Framing

In alignment with high academic standards, the MindEase team explicitly clarifies what the current prototype demonstrates:

  • Preference-Led, Not Diagnostic: Onboarding accommodates learner-stated preferences (e.g. "I prefer visual explanations and shorter sections"). It does not attempt to diagnose psychological conditions or claim universal comprehension improvements.
  • Active Ratio vs Attention: The displayed Focus Score in the dashboard represents the mathematical ratio of active dwell time to total session duration (filtering out idle periods >5 min). It is a passive activity proxy, not a direct neural measure of human attention or cognitive mastery.
  • Strict Non-Destructive Invariance: Generated adapted lessons are always paired with the exact verbatim original source blocks, allowing the reader to inspect the author’s original wording at any moment.
